Addendum 20260709

English definitions proclaim the French as “Latin,” but not “Latino.” Weird? Screwed-up language?

I think that proclaiming myself as “Latino Asian” is more accurate than “Filipino.” What about “Latin Asian,” then? It’s not “Whatever!”

My language tastes change day to day. I do have multilingual variety, not available to many people. I did meet this Oriental girl in Wat Yanviriya, a Thai Buddhist temple, when I was attending meditation classes: She’s fluent in Vietnamese, Mandarin, Cantonese, and English. What a collection she has!

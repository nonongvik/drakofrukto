20260512 ‘Viktor’ in Japan
Viktor Nonong Medrano

My actual name “Victor” one renders differently depending on language source to Japanese: ビクトル “Bikutoru” from Spanish, ビクター “Bikutā” from English, ヴィクトル “Vikutoru” from Russian, and ヴィクトール “Vikutōru” from German. “Biku” is for short, which rhymes with the word 比丘 for “Buddhist monk.”

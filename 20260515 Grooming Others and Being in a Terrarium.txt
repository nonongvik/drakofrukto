20260515 Grooming Others and Being in a Terrarium
Viktor Nonong Medrano

I think that it’s no use for Yankees trying to groom Chinese and East Indians. For all we know, the future may not be human.

People around are just promoting natural human languages. It may be that Artificial Intelligence in the future may create new languages for people to use.

I don’t understand the patronage of Anglophiles to English. English is a highly corrupted language. In the future, it may just become a museum piece, as people find better alternatives.

Why was Tagalog chosen for Global Xenoglossia? Is there some kind of reason? Medicinal? Indigenous Vindication? Spiritual Renewal?

There are things happening today in which we may never know the explanations. Are we like pets of Higher Beings? Is Earth like a big terrarium?

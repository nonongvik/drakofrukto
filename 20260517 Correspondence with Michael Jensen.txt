20260517 Correspondence with Michael Jensen
Viktor Nonong Medrano

Thanks for sharing, Michael! Your experience as an astronomer must have been really a special part of your life. I remember as a boy in the outskirts of Quezon City. As I told you, it was like Arizona or the Oz Outback. The fields of soil were red with tall grasses. At night, we kids, my cousins, brother, and I, would see meteorites fall from the sky. At day times, we looked for tektites. Each field of red soil we named after a planet: Mars, Venus, Mercury, et cetera. Whirlwinds or dust devils sometimes as big as a house would pass by sometimes. I tried to run to catch them to stand in the middle of the vortices. There were strange plants, like purple-leaved vines.

20260517 Tagalog as a Bridge
Viktor Nonong Medrano

Tagalog is like a bridge. Not only does it have Spanish loanwords, but also English, bridging different worlds in the West. Tagalog has many indigenous Austronesian (“South Islands”) words and some words from the Sinosphere, bridging different worlds in the East.

Some of us Filipino linguists think that Tagalog has three components analogous to components in Japanese writing: The English loanwords are like Kanji, logograms, with their opaque orthographies. Native words are like Hiragana with their transparent orthographies, whilst Hispanic loanwords are like Katakana with their also transparent orthographies.

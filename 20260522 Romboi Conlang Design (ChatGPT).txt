CHATGPT:

Lingua: Romboi

A logical constructed language inspired by Lojban, but with Romance-derived roots and highly transparent spelling.

The goal of Romboi is:

culturally Romance in flavor,
logically precise,
phonetic and regular,
compact like Lojban,
emotionally warmer and more melodic.

⸻

1. Core Design Principles

A. One Sound = One Letter

Orthography is completely transparent.

Letter	Sound	Example
a	/a/	Spanish casa
e	/e/	Italian bene
i	/i/	machine
o	/o/	Italian sole
u	/u/	rule
c	/ʃ/	“sh”
j	/ʒ/	French jour
x	/x/	Spanish jota
y	/j/	yes
r	tapped/trilled	Romance r
q	/ŋ/	sing

No silent letters.
Stress always falls on the penultimate syllable.

⸻

2. Root Structure

All content roots are exactly 5 letters.

These are called radis.

They are algorithmically derived from Romance vocabulary.

Formation Method

Choose a Romance etymon.
Remove inflections.
Compress consonants.
Preserve recognizable vowels.
Normalize spelling.

Examples:

Romance Source	Meaning	Romboi Root
parlare / parlar	speak	parla
vedere / ver	see	vedra
sapere / saber	know	sapra
mangiare / manger	eat	manja
amare / amar	love	amora
dormire	sleep	dorma
pensare	think	pensa
vivere	live	vivae
venire	come	venia
andare	go	andra

Every root belongs to a semantic predicate frame, like Lojban’s gismu.

3. Predicate Logic Structure

Every root expresses a relation.

Example

parla

Means:

x1 speaks language x2 to x3 about x4

Example:

mi parla lo romboi tu

“I speak Romboi to you.”

Word	Meaning
mi	I/me
lo	descriptor marker
romboi	Romboi language
tu	you

4. Grammar

Word Classes

No noun/verb distinction.

Every root is fundamentally a predicate.

mi amora tu

= “I love you.”

Literally:

I am-in-a-love-relation with you.

5. Particles

Pronouns

Romboi	Meaning
mi	I
tu	you
il	he/she
nu	we
vu	you all
li	they

Tense

Particle	Meaning
pa	past
fu	future
na	present/neutral

mi pa dorma

“I slept.”

Negation

Particle	Meaning
no	logical negation

mi no sapra

“I do not know.”

6. Logical Connectives

Highly Lojbanic.

Word	Meaning
e	and
o	inclusive or
xo	if
xa	iff
ni	not

Example:

xo tu venia mi andra

“If you come, I go.

7. Argument Markers

Like Lojban place structures, but simpler.

Marker	Role
ga	agent
de	object
ko	instrument
bi	location
su	cause

Example:

ga mi manja de pano bi roma

“I eat bread in Rome.”

8. Questions

Yes/No

Sentence-final particle:

tu sapra qo

“Do you know?”

Wh-words

Word	Meaning
qui	who
que	what
ubi	where
cando	when
proce	why
como	how

9. Compounds

Roots may combine transparently.

Example

Compound	Meaning
amopar	love-speech / poetry
vedlux	light-source
saptec	science
dormox	dream

10. Derivational Morphology

Regular suffixes:

Suffix	Meaning
-i	abstract noun
-a	predicate root
-or	agent
-um	tool
-ia	field/domain

Examples:

Word	Meaning
pensi	thought
pensor	thinker
vidum	visual tool
sapia	science/knowledge-domain

11. Phonotactics

Allowed syllables:

(C)(C)V(C)

Allowed initial clusters:

pr

fl
br
dr
gl

The language intentionally sounds Mediterranean and fluid.

12. Example Text

Romboi

mi amora la stela.
nu pensa que li vivae inter stara.
fu tempo, nu vedra altra mundi.

English

I love the stars.
We think that they live among the stars.
In the future, we will see other worlds.

14. Distinctive Flavor

Unlike Lojban, Romboi:

sounds romantic and human,
preserves etymological familiarity,
allows emotional resonance,
remains machine-parsable,
avoids ambiguity through particles and argument marking.

It feels somewhat like:

Italian + Occitan + Lojban,
with hints of Esperanto regularity.

15. Example Conversation

A: tu parla romboi qo?
B: si. mi sapra poca.
A: bona! nu canta e bibra vino.

English:

A: Do you speak Romboi?
B: Yes. I know a little.
A: Good! Let us sing and drink wine.

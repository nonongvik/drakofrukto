Addendum 20260713

In conversing with Artificial Intelligence in recent years, I learn that there seems to be an ongoing paradigm shift about deep space or outer space, becoming relegated to post-biology. The AI seems to think that the ideas from older sci-fi to which I’ve been exposed have become “vintage.” This “vintage sci-fi” often described future humans in deep space, in outer space, or on other planets or moons, or in space stations. The AI now underscores the frailty of human physiology and psychology for such dangerous explorations. Many people still persevere with the hope that humans would extend themselves beyond Earth. Maybe, it’s not impossible, but now improbable. In any case, transhumanism, the modification of the human body and mind via various technologies, may be key.

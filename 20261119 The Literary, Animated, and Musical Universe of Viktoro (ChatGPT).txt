1. The world of books and their authors
SPECULATIVE FICTION · SCIENCE FICTION · FANTASY · LITERATURE

Samuel R. Delany: language, civilization, and the future

Samuel R. Delany
Delany is one of Viktoro's particularly important science-fiction authors. His works explore language, identity, social organization, sexuality, and the complexities of technologically advanced societies.

Among his works are:
	•	Babel-17 — especially fascinating because of its relationship between language, thought, and perception.
	•	Nova — a spacefaring civilization, technological ambition, and the search for an extraordinary resource.
	•	Trouble on Triton — an exploration of social alternatives, identity, and unconventional futures.

Delany's work is especially relevant to Viktoro's interests as a linguist and conlanger. The possibility that language can influence how people understand reality is an especially compelling subject.

Ursula K. Le Guin: anthropology among the stars

Ursula K. Le Guin
Le Guin is among the authors whose imagination resonates particularly strongly with Viktoro's interest in cultures, anthropology, and alternative civilizations.
Her Always Coming Home is especially significant. It imagines a future society through its stories, customs, poetry, music, beliefs, and ways of living.

Viktoro owns the French edition, La Vallée de l'éternel retour, and is reading the Spanish edition, El eterno regreso a casa. He has also expressed an interest in acquiring the Japanese edition.

This is more than an interest in reading a novel in several languages. It is an opportunity to encounter the same imagined civilization through different linguistic and cultural perspectives.

Le Guin's other works, particularly the Hainish Cycle, also complement his fascination with extraterrestrial societies and cultural diversity.

Robert Silverberg: the magnificent world of Majipoor

Robert Silverberg
Silverberg's Majipoor Chronicles is another important part of Viktoro's literary universe.

Viktoro has read the work in English and Spanish. He particularly enjoys the extraordinary atmosphere of Majipoor: its immense landscapes, alien creatures, unusual societies, and imaginative descriptions of life.

The Spanish edition, Crónicas de Majipur, accompanies him during his visits to Tim Hortons.

The poetic descriptions of alien jungles and strange creatures, sometimes involving seemingly nonsensical syllables, are especially appealing to someone who enjoys language invention.

Galaxy Express 999
This is one of the most revealing choices in his anime collection.
The story's central premise involves travelling through space to obtain a mechanical body, potentially replacing biological existence with a much longer-lasting artificial form.

Viktoro finds this concept intriguing because it raises questions about transhumanism, mortality, identity, and the possibility of overcoming biological limitations.

The search for a mechanical body is not simply a technological adventure. It raises a profound question: if humanity can escape biological mortality, what might be gained or lost?
